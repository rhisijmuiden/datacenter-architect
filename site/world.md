## Day, night and seasons

The sun follows the clock and the time of year: long days and a high sun in June, short and low in December. At night office windows glow, lamp posts and facade lights switch on and trucks drive with their headlights on. Spring brings blossom, summer deep green, autumn orange leaves and winter bare trees and sometimes snow that stays a few days.

## Weather

Sun, clouds, rain, thunderstorms with lightning, snow and fog, changing every half day. Every savegame has its own fixed weather, so loading a game gives the same weather again. Next to the clock you see the weather, temperature and season; the tooltip is the forecast.

With **weather plays along** switched on at the start of a game, the weather also counts: cold days give free cooling, a heatwave takes 20% off your heat rejection, storms cause power cuts more often, snow and fog slow trucks down and clouds dim your solar panels. Off, it is just for looks.

The **sun** and **cloud** buttons top right switch day/night and weather on or off at any time (also under Options › Graphics). Off means a sunny summer afternoon.

## Walk around

Press <kbd>F</kbd> (or the walker button top right) to walk through your datacenter yourself. <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd> walk, <kbd>Shift</kbd> runs, the mouse looks around, <kbd>Page Up</kbd>/<kbd>Page Down</kbd> take the stairs or a lift and <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>F</kbd> brings you back. You walk through doors but not through walls, fences or racks, and the servers get louder as you come closer. Just for fun.

## Photo mode and timelapse

- **Photo mode** (<kbd>F9</kbd> or the camera button): the interface disappears and the clock stops. Choose the time of day for the photo, one of seven filters, depth of field, and a **cinematic camera** that floats slowly around the campus. <kbd>F12</kbd> or <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves a photo to Pictures/Datacenter Architect.
- **Timelapse**: every 7 game days at noon a camera high above takes a picture of the whole campus. Play them back as a film under Progress › Timelapse.

## Progress

Everything you achieve is tracked under **Progress** (<kbd>F8</kbd>):

- **Statistics**: money, income and costs, contract value, power use, PUE, uptime, racks, customers, traffic, reputation, ESG and staff, in charts over 30 days, a year or everything.
- **Scenarios with medals**: pick one at New game. *Quick start* (racks in use within a year), *Rescue a bankrupt datacenter*, *10 MW in 5 years*, *Green giant* (ESG) and *Every watt counts* (PUE). Bronze, silver and gold; gold wins the scenario at once, and you keep playing after.
- **Achievements**: close to forty, from racks and megawatts to N+1, PUE, certificates, uptime streaks and the fun ones (photographer, tourist). They are kept in a profile over all your savegames.
- **Datacenter of the year**: on New Year's Day a jury picks the best datacenter of the past year on reputation, uptime, PUE, ESG, growth and size, against your rivals. A top-three place puts a gold, silver or bronze statuette on the desk in the director's office.

## Your own brand

Under **Company › Brand** you rename the company and design a logo: six shapes, a main colour, a colour for letters and border, and up to three letters. The logo appears on facade signs, on the sides of the equipment trucks, on the vests of your own technicians and on every rack door. Contractors keep their own colours, so you can see who works for you.
