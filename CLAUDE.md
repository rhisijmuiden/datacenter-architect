# Datacenter Architect: working notes

- Offline Windows 11 game, Godot 4 with C#. No mail server and no remote backups (deliberate; offline game). Local savegames only.
- Default language English; every player-facing string must also have a Dutch translation. Keep technical terms (rack, CRAH, UPS, N+1) untranslated.
- Branches: `main` = stable channel, `test` = test channel. Work lands on `test` first, then is promoted to `main`.
- Follow the brand in `brand/BRAND.md` and `brand/tokens.json`. Power = amber, cooling = cyan, fiber = orange, everywhere.
- The in-game build menu is a bar along the bottom of the screen (categories with icons, item tray above), the bulldozer (demolish) sits bottom-right. Reinder asked for this on 2026-11-19; menus inside dialogs (Options) keep their tabs on the left.
- Player settings live in `Core/AppSettings.cs` (user://settings.cfg) and are edited in `UI/OptionsDialog.cs`; display logic in `Core/DisplaySettings.cs`, audio buses in `Core/AudioMixer.cs`.
- Game rules live in `docs/game-design.md`; update it when gameplay decisions change.
- Godot project lives in `game/` (Godot 4.5.1 .NET, C#, net8.0). Build: `dotnet build game/DatacenterArchitect.csproj`; tests: `godot --headless --path game -- --run-tests`.
- Player-facing text goes through keys in `game/locale/strings.tsv` (key, en, nl). The translation test fails when a Dutch text is missing.
- Releases: tag `vX.Y.Z-test.N` on `test` for the test channel, `vX.Y.Z` on `main` for stable; `.github/workflows/release.yml` builds the installer.
- Step 7 staff, maintenance and events: `Sim/Staff.cs` (roles, automatic hiring, policies, plans), `Sim/Ops.cs` (wear, faults, repairs, spare parts, random events), `Sim/SimStaff.cs` (staff figures on site), `UI/StaffPanel.cs`. Design rule from Reinder: no micromanagement. Managers hire, roster and train by themselves; the player only sets policies and service plans. Tests use a demo site with events off (`Quiet()` in TestRunner) so they stay deterministic.
- Step 8 security, research and reputation: `Sim/Security.cs` (security level, policies, certificates), `Sim/Research.cs` (research tree, lab, effects, office rental), `Sim/Reputation.cs` (quality, reviews, proposals), `UI/ReputationPanel.cs`. Save format 7. Screenshot flags `--rep-demo --reputation N`.
- Step 9 growth: `Sim/Market.cs` (rivals, news, acquisitions, IPO), `Sim/Sites.cs` (campus land via `Grow`, countries, remote sites, office leases to customers first), `Sim/Energy.cs` (spot prices, solar/wind/battery, water, ESG), `Sim/Risk.cs` (cyber/SOC, disasters, insurance, supply delays), `Sim/Hosting.cs` (own hosting company + warehouse chain), `Sim/KeyStaff.cs`, `Sim/Growth.cs` (daily turn + money lines), `UI/GrowthPanel.cs`, news ticker in `UI/Hud.cs`. Growth uses its own RNG (`GrowthRng`) so older tests stay deterministic. Save format 8; multi-arg notes use the separator `¦`. Screenshot flags `--growth-demo --growth N`.
- Step 10 looks: `Sim/Climate.cs` (seasons, sun, deterministic weather per half day, weather effects behind the new-game switch `WeatherEffects`, forecast), `World/WorldView.Sky.cs` (sun/moon, sky, fog, rain/snow particles, lightning, seasonal and night materials), `World/FirstPerson.cs` (walker, uses `Nav.CanStep`), `Sim/Migrations.cs` (moving-truck convoys in and out), round people in `Models.Person`. Lights that switch on at night go in the group `Models.NightLightGroup`. Save format 9 (old saves: weather for looks only). Screenshot flags `--time 2027-12-20T21:00 --weather snow --snow 1 --walk x,z,floor,yaw,pitch --migration [--movers] --cables [--at-work]`. Engineers carry a cable reel (`Agent.Cable`, amber power or orange fiber) and unroll it at the rack.
- GUI v2 (playtest batch 1): management rail on the left (`UI/Sections.cs` defines sections F1-F7 and their tabs; screens can appear under another section's tabs), screens open docked as a drawer via `Modal.Dock` (Main.OpenPanel / OpenSectionTab). New screens: add a `PanelKind`, override `SelectTab`, register own tab column with `OwnTabs`. Overview screen in `UI/OverviewPanel.cs`. Playtest feedback list: /mnt/project-files/datacenter-game/playtest/feedback.md (project files, not in repo).
- Parking bays: `Sim/ParkingLayout.cs` (world lines + drag preview). Trucks follow `Nav.CenterLane`. Cranes per site: `WorldView.CranesFor`. Screenshot flag `--showcase [--close]` shows a big site, parking and the three trucks; `--section N[,tab]` opens the drawer.
- Playtest batch 2: people have human proportions (`Models.Person`, slim/broad builds, jeans, jackets); trucks are articulated (`Models.Truck` node "Trailer" pivots at the kingpin, `WorldView.FollowTrailer`), dock parking uses `Simulation.TruckRear`; site cranes are mobile self-erecting cranes; lighting is filmic with a world-space mottle (`WorldView.Grain`) on grass and paving. Screenshot flag `--showcase --lineup` shows one of each person. Next step decided by Reinder: replace primitive figures with real CC0 3D models (own phase/thread).
