# Datacenter Architect: working notes

- Offline Windows 11 game, Godot 4 with C#. No mail server and no remote backups (deliberate; offline game). Local savegames only.
- Default language English; every player-facing string must also have a Dutch translation. Keep technical terms (rack, CRAH, UPS, N+1) untranslated.
- Branches: `main` = stable channel, `test` = test channel. Work lands on `test` first, then is promoted to `main`.
- Follow the brand in `brand/BRAND.md` and `brand/tokens.json`. Power = amber, cooling = cyan, fiber = orange, everywhere.
- The in-game build menu is a bar along the bottom of the screen (categories with icons, item tray above), the bulldozer (demolish) sits bottom-right. Reinder asked for this on 2026-11-19; menus inside dialogs (Options) keep their tabs on the left.
- Player settings live in `Core/AppSettings.cs` (user://settings.cfg) and are edited in `UI/OptionsDialog.cs`; display logic in `Core/DisplaySettings.cs`, audio buses in `Core/AudioMixer.cs`.
- Game rules live in `docs/game-design.md`; update it when gameplay decisions change.
- Godot project lives in `game/` (Godot 4.5.1 .NET, C#, net8.0). Build: `dotnet build game/DatacenterArchitect.csproj`; tests: `godot --headless --path game -- --run-tests`.
- Player-facing text goes through keys in `game/locale/strings.tsv` (key, en, nl). The translation test fails when a Dutch text is missing.
- Releases: tag `vX.Y.Z-test.N` on `test` for the test channel, `vX.Y.Z` on `main` for stable; `.github/workflows/release.yml` builds the installer.
- Step 7 staff, maintenance and events: `Sim/Staff.cs` (roles, automatic hiring, policies, plans), `Sim/Ops.cs` (wear, faults, repairs, spare parts, random events), `Sim/SimStaff.cs` (staff figures on site), `UI/StaffPanel.cs`. Design rule from Reinder: no micromanagement. Managers hire, roster and train by themselves; the player only sets policies and service plans. Tests use a demo site with events off (`Quiet()` in TestRunner) so they stay deterministic.
- Step 8 security, research and reputation: `Sim/Security.cs` (security level, policies, certificates), `Sim/Research.cs` (research tree, lab, effects, office rental), `Sim/Reputation.cs` (quality, reviews, proposals), `UI/ReputationPanel.cs`. Save format 7. Screenshot flags `--rep-demo --reputation N`.
- Step 9 growth: `Sim/Market.cs` (rivals, news, acquisitions, IPO), `Sim/Sites.cs` (campus land via `Grow`, countries, remote sites, office leases to customers first), `Sim/Energy.cs` (spot prices, solar/wind/battery, water, ESG), `Sim/Risk.cs` (cyber/SOC, disasters, insurance, supply delays), `Sim/Hosting.cs` (own hosting company + warehouse chain), `Sim/KeyStaff.cs`, `Sim/Growth.cs` (daily turn + money lines), `UI/GrowthPanel.cs`, news ticker in `UI/Hud.cs`. Growth uses its own RNG (`GrowthRng`) so older tests stay deterministic. Save format 8; multi-arg notes use the separator `¦`. Screenshot flags `--growth-demo --growth N`.
- Step 10 looks: `Sim/Climate.cs` (seasons, sun, deterministic weather per half day, weather effects behind the new-game switch `WeatherEffects`, forecast), `World/WorldView.Sky.cs` (sun/moon, sky, fog, rain/snow particles, lightning, seasonal and night materials), `World/FirstPerson.cs` (walker, uses `Nav.CanStep`), `Sim/Migrations.cs` (moving-truck convoys in and out), round people in `Models.Person`. Lights that switch on at night go in the group `Models.NightLightGroup`. Save format 9 (old saves: weather for looks only). Screenshot flags `--time 2027-12-20T21:00 --weather snow --snow 1 --walk x,z,floor,yaw,pitch --migration [--movers] --cables [--at-work]`. Engineers carry a cable reel (`Agent.Cable`, amber power or orange fiber) and unroll it at the rack.
- GUI v2 (playtest batch 1): management rail on the left (`UI/Sections.cs` defines sections F1-F8 and their tabs; screens can appear under another section's tabs), screens open docked as a drawer via `Modal.Dock` (Main.OpenPanel / OpenSectionTab). New screens: add a `PanelKind`, override `SelectTab`, register own tab column with `OwnTabs`. Overview screen in `UI/OverviewPanel.cs`. Playtest feedback list: /mnt/project-files/datacenter-game/playtest/feedback.md (project files, not in repo).
- Parking bays: `Sim/ParkingLayout.cs` (world lines + drag preview). Trucks follow `Nav.CenterLane`. Cranes per site: `WorldView.CranesFor`. Screenshot flag `--showcase [--close]` shows a big site, parking and the three trucks; `--section N[,tab]` opens the drawer.
- Playtest batch 2: people have human proportions (`Models.Person`, slim/broad builds, jeans, jackets); trucks are articulated (`Models.Truck` node "Trailer" pivots at the kingpin, `WorldView.FollowTrailer`), dock parking uses `Simulation.TruckRear`; site cranes are mobile self-erecting cranes; lighting is filmic with a world-space mottle (`WorldView.Grain`) on grass and paving. Screenshot flag `--showcase --lineup` shows one of each person. Next step decided by Reinder: replace primitive figures with real CC0 3D models (own phase/thread).
- People are rigged CC0 models (Quaternius Modular Men/Women) in `game/assets/models/people/`, built by `World/People.cs` (`PersonFigure`: outfit per role, recoloured clothes/skin/hair, swapped heads; animation advanced by hand in `WorldView.SyncAgents`, arms posed on top for Carry/Push/Reel; toolbox on the `Wrist.R` bone). Credits and the prep pipeline (`tools/models/`) in `game/assets/models/CREDITS.md`. Every new third-party model goes in that credits table.
- Floor links: `Catalog.IsShaft` (freight `Lift`, `PassengerLift`, `Stairs`) span all floors of a building; `Catalog.ShaftAllows` lets walkers use all three and carried loads only the freight lift; `Nav.Find` picks the cheapest link (`Nav.LinkCost`). `Models.Shaft(kind, top)` draws them on the floor in view. Screenshot flag `--floor N`.
- Undo/redo: `Sim/Undo.cs` (`UndoHistory`) records each player build action as a diff (added/removed buildings, rooms, doors, objects, curves; changed surface/fence cells; money). `Main.ApplyDrag/PlaceObject/PlaceDoor/CurveClick` wrap their change in `UndoHistory.Take` + `Record`. New player build actions must do the same. Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y (or Ctrl+Shift+Z), buttons above the bulldozer.
- Smart drag: `GameState.RowBlock` turns a wide rack drag into pairs of rows (front, 1 m cold aisle, front; 2 m hot aisle between pairs; R starts with the backs); the ghost shows count, rows, size, kW and cost; erase drags show size and object count. Screenshot flag `--drag-demo`.
- Real 3D world (2026-11-20): `World/Assets.cs` loads models from `game/assets/models` as one merged mesh per model (`Assets.Mesh`, `Place`, `Centered`, `Many` for MultiMesh, `Mount` keeps `Fan*` nodes spinning). Plants use the wind shader in `Foliage` (seasons recolour it). Equipment, street furniture, fences, trees and grass are our own Blender-script models (`tools/models/blender/`, output `assets/models/made/`); furniture, cars and neighbour buildings are Kenney CC0. Ground and wall photo textures (Poly Haven) in `assets/textures/ground/` via `WorldView.Ground.cs` (`SurfaceMat`, `WallMat`); textures must import as VRAM compressed with mipmaps (edit their `.import`). Forest/meadow/parked cars/neighbours in `WorldView.Plants.cs`. Options: lighting quality (SSIL/SDFGI) and grass toggle. Site vehicles (`Models.MadeTruck/MadeExcavator/MadeCrane`, `tools/models/blender/vehicles.py`) and gate/barrier/turbine (`Models.MadeMoving`, `site.py`) are built from per-joint .glb parts on the same animated nodes (Trailer, Cargo, Jib, Cable, Arm, Leaf, FanRotor). The old primitive (box) versions of models that now have a real model were removed (2026-11-20); only objects without a model file still use primitives in `Models.Thing`. Every new model goes in `assets/models/CREDITS.md`.
- Step 11 progress (round 1): `Sim/Stats.cs` (daily `StatSample`, stored as text in `StatLog`, thinned to 800; `GameId`), `Sim/Achievements.cs` (`Achievements.All`, `GameState.Unlock`), `Sim/Scenarios.cs` (`Scenarios.All`, `Scenarios.Start`, medal per day), `Core/Profile.cs` (achievements and best medals over all saves, user://profile.cfg), `UI/ProgressPanel.cs` + `UI/LineChart.cs` (section F8), `World/Timelapse.cs` (SubViewport picture every 7 game days at noon, user://timelapse/{GameId}), `UI/PhotoMode.cs` (F9; filters, DOF, cinematic camera). Texts of this step live in `game/locale/progress.tsv` (loaded next to strings.tsv via `Loc.ExtraFiles`). Hooks: `GameState.Advance` calls `StatsTick`/`ProgressDay`, `Book` calls `LedgerDay`. Screenshot flags `--progress-demo --section 7,N` and `--photo`.
- Step 11 round 2: grid congestion queue `Sim/GridQueue.cs` (`NewGameOptions.GridQueue`, `GridQueueDays`, queue before the lead time), Datacenter of the year `Sim/Awards.cs` (`AwardsDay` on New Year's Day, `Judge`, `AwardMarks`, statuettes via `Models.Statuette` on `GameState.TrophyDesk`, Progress tab `TabAwards`), tutorial coach `Sim/Tutorial.cs` + `UI/TutorialCoach.cs` (`NewGameOptions.Tutorial`, follows the goal checklist; tips `tut_*`), plan mode `Sim/Plan.cs` + `UI/PlanBar.cs` + `World/WorldView.Plan.cs` (key B; `GameState.Planning` makes building free while drawing, `MarkPlanned` after the action, `Planned` ids skipped by `Simulation.Sites`, the delivery list and `FinishEverything`; `BuildPlan` / `DiscardPlan` clear the undo history). Texts in `game/locale/progress.tsv`. Screenshot flags `--plan-demo`, `--award-demo [--close]`, `--tutorial N`.
- Step 11 round 3: company logo `Sim/CompanyLogo.cs` (`GameState.Logo`, `Rename`, `CompanyLogo.For(name)` for new and old saves), drawn by `World/LogoArt.cs` (`LogoView` control; `LogoArt` SubViewport texture + shared `Material`, `LogoArt.Quad`), placed by `World/WorldView.Logo.cs` (facade sign, equipment-truck livery, `People.CompanyColor` for own staff, `RackRenderer.LogoKey` on rack doors); editor `UI/CompanyPanel.Brand.cs` (Company › Brand). Audio: generative music `World/Music.cs` (`MusicMood` per area, bus `AudioMixer.Music`, `AppSettings.MusicVolume/MusicOn`), area layers in `World/Ambience.cs` (`Outdoor`, `Office`, `Site`, `Daylight`), set by `Main.AreaSound`. Screenshot flag `--brand-demo [facade|racks]`.
- Security (2026-11-20): the updater (`Core/UpdateService.cs`) only accepts HTTPS links into this repository's GitHub releases and installer names `DatacenterArchitect-<version>-Setup.exe`, and checks size + SHA-256 (GitHub asset `digest`, else the `<installer>.sha256` asset that release.yml uploads) before it runs anything. Saves are read through `SaveSystem.Read` (size cap, `Plausible`, no exceptions) and written atomically via `.tmp`. `GameState.GameId` only accepts letters/digits (it names a folder). Workflows: actions pinned by commit SHA, Godot downloads checked against pinned SHA-512, workflow inputs passed via `env:`, least-privilege `permissions`. When bumping Godot, update the SHA-512 values in both workflows.
- Pathfinding: walks to a single cell use `Simulation.GoTo` (A* + `Nav.Connected` area labels); multi-cell goals use `Go`. Keep new walker code off full-site searches for unreachable spots; check with `--bench`.
- Playtest round 3: roads 6 m or wider get a dashed centre line and traffic keeps right (`Nav.TwoLaneWidth`, `Nav.CenterLane`; trucks store `Agent.Route` and drive back via `Simulation.WayBack`). Racks need a free cell in front and behind inside the hall (`GameState.RackAccess`, `BlocksRack`); the placement ghost shows a front arrow and cyan/orange aisle tiles. Fiber trays are U-channels with waterfalls, only over placed racks (`WorldView.HallDecor`). Loading bays: one per roll-up door (`Simulation.Docks`, `FreeBays`, up to `MaxDockTrucks` at once), drawn by `WorldView.DockBays` (lines, number, bumpers). R&D budget `GameState.ResearchBudget` (`ResearchTree.BudgetPerDay/BudgetSpeed`, paid only on lab days). Commuting `Sim/Commute.cs` (`Commute.Preferred`, bike places in `BikeRack`/`BikeShed`, `EvCharger` bays drawn green with "EV" in `WorldView.ParkedCars`; cyclists ride in, `Agent.Ride`, `PersonPose.Ride`; parked bikes `WorldView.Commute.cs`). Office interiors `Sim/Interior.cs` (`AutoInterior`, `AutoFurnish` on `RoomFinished`, `Room.Style`/`StyleOf`, `OfficeNeeds` checklist in the inspector) drawn by `WorldView.Interior.cs` (feature wall, art or dashboards, desk screens, rug). Own models in `tools/models/blender/commute.py` (bikes, shed, charger; Blender via `pip install bpy`). Thumbnails use a studio light, podium with system-colour rim, photo-textured ground and furnished room corners. Screenshot flags `--road-demo`, `--ghost-demo x,z`, `--commute-demo [--bikes|--ev]`, `--office-demo [--close]`, `--build-menu cat_x`.
